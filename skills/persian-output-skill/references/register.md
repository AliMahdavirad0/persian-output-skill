# Voice and register

Choose register from the intended deliverable and audience, not merely the casual wording of the request. An informal request to translate a research abstract still calls for an academic abstract. Use the source register and the user's explicit preference; ask only when unresolved ambiguity materially changes the result.

| Register | Intended use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conversational | Personal messages, spoken dialogue | «می‌تونی فایل رو دوباره بفرستی؟» |
| Clear professional | Product explanations, web articles, ordinary work email | «لطفاً فایل را دوباره بفرستید.» |
| Formal | Formal correspondence and notices | «خواهشمند است فایل را دوباره ارسال کنید.» |
| Academic | Papers and technical argument | «این روش ممکن است خطا را در شرایط آزمون کاهش دهد.» |

Maintain one register unless the source deliberately changes voice, such as quoted dialogue. Natural Persian is not synonymous with casual Persian. Avoid adding marketing enthusiasm to neutral technical text or making conversation bureaucratic.

Prefer clear verbs and natural collocations, but do not ban passive voice or words such as «می‌باشد» universally. Review whether they fit this sentence and genre. Preserve authorial emphasis, meaningful repetition, uncertainty, and emotional tone. Do not describe stylistic revision as proof that a text was or was not AI-generated.

For polishing, improve rhythm and clause order while preserving the writer's meaning. Do not delete important details just because a shorter sentence sounds smoother.
